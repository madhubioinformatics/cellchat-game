How to play CellChat: The Game
🎯 Objective
Complete all 18 missions before the 45-minute timer runs out. Each mission asks a neuroscience question — answer correctly to earn points and keep your streak alive.
🧪 Your Cell
You play as one of 11 Swarup Lab members, each mapped to a unique cell type. Your cell is marked YOU on stage and in the roster.
📋 Missions
Each mission shows a narration from one cell to another, then a 4-choice science question. Read the narration carefully — it always contains a clue.
⚡ Speed Bonus
Answer within 3 seconds to earn an extra +50 points. Watch for the ⚡ icon on your score popup.
🔥 Streak Bonus
Each consecutive correct answer adds streak × 20 bonus points. One wrong answer resets your streak to ×1.
🏆 Scoring
L1: 100pts  L2: 150pts  L3: 175pts  L4: 225pts  L5: 250–300pts. Plus speed and streak bonuses.
