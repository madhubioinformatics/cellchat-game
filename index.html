How to play CellChat: The Game
Objective
Complete all missions before the 45-minute timer runs out. Answer neuroscience questions correctly to earn points and keep your streak alive.
Your Cell
You play as one of 11 Swarup Lab members. Your cell is marked YOU on stage and in the roster.
Missions
Each mission shows a narration from one cell to another, then a 4-choice question. The narration always contains a clue.
Speed Bonus
Answer within 3 seconds for an extra +50 points.
Streak Bonus
Each consecutive correct answer adds streak x20 bonus. One wrong answer resets to x1.
Scoring
L1: 100pts   L2: 150pts   L3: 175pts   L4: 225pts   L5: 250-300pts. Plus speed and streak bonuses on top.
