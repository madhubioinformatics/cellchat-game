## Play Live
**https://madhubioinformatics.github.io/cellchat-game/**

## About
CellChat: The Game puts you inside a living brain simulation.
Choose one of 11 Swarup Lab members as your cell, select a brain disorder
to focus on, and answer molecular neuroscience questions to keep the brain alive.

## Features
- 🧬 11 playable Swarup Lab members (Madhu, Negin, Nellie, Luis, Ze, Kristen, Nora, Veronica, Das, Johnny, Swarup)
- 🎮 5 game levels: The Awakening → Synaptic Storm → Crisis Mode → Disease Ward → Swarup's Lab
- 🧩 4 brain disorder focus modes: Alzheimer's · Parkinson's · Schizophrenia · Autism
- 💡 Hints system with scientific explanations
- 🏆 12 achievements, XP progression, streak bonuses
- 📖 7-step interactive tutorial
- 🔬 25 neuroscience missions with real molecular detail
